1. The Problem We Solved

Marketing teams and creative agencies produce content faster than ever using generative AI and digital design tools. However, publishing content without a centralized review guardrail causes serious brand safety and legal problems:

  • Off-Palette Visuals: Unapproved colors and outdated logos make marketing campaigns look inconsistent and unprofessional.
  • Unreadable Contrast: Low-contrast text on image backgrounds violates web accessibility standards (WCAG) and makes ads hard to read.
  • Unsubstantiated Claims: Overpromising phrases like "guarantees 100% compliance" or "unrivaled market leader" create severe legal liabilities and false advertising fines.
  • Manual Review Bottlenecks: Passing drafts back and forth between designers, legal teams, and brand managers takes 3 to 5 days, slowing down marketing launches.
2. What We Made (The OmniGuard Solution)

OmniGuard AI is an automated brand safety firewall for outbound marketing. Before any ad copy or creative image is published, OmniGuard AI scans it in under a second against official brand guidelines. Instead of just acting as a blocker that says "no", OmniGuard AI explains why an issue was flagged and automatically generates one-click smart fixes that preserve the creator's original marketing message.

Who Uses OmniGuard AI & Why It Matters
User Persona Their Biggest Challenge How OmniGuard AI Solves It
Content Creators & Marketers Waiting days for approvals and getting vague feedback on drafts. Instant line-by-line checks with 1-click fixes to make drafts compliant in seconds.
Brand & Design Leads Ensuring consistent colors, fonts, and messaging across all channels. Automated color matching and tone checks against approved master brand manuals.
Legal & Compliance Officers Need auditable proof that every claim is verified before release. Immutable approval ledger, multi-role sign-offs, and verifiable digital certificates.
The 5-Stage Live Product Workflow
Pipeline Stage What the System Does What the User Sees and Does
1. Ingest Stores and indexes company brand guidelines and fact sheets. Upload company guideline PDFs, policy manuals, or claim sheets with one click.
2. Multi-Audit Scans text copy and visual creative images simultaneously. Paste copy and upload an image; the scanner checks tone, claims, color, and contrast.
3. Smart Fix Generates pinpoint text fixes that keep the original meaning. Review side-by-side suggestions and click "Apply All Fixes" to update copy instantly.
4. Governance Manages department sign-offs (Brand, Legal, Security). Authorized team members review and approve audits, generating signed certificates.
5. Analytics Monitors policy violations and recurring mistakes over time. View visual charts showing which channels have the most issues and where rules are unclear.

3. What We Built in Round 2 (Delivered Capabilities)

In Round 2, we took our initial prototype and built a complete, production-ready enterprise brand governance platform. Below are the core capabilities fully working in the live platform today:

1. Real-Time Multi-Modal Scanner (Text Copy + Visual Creatives)
Instead of checking only text, OmniGuard AI inspects draft copy and visual images in parallel. It checks marketing text for overhyped buzzwords and false guarantees, while simultaneously examining the creative image to ensure the visual elements match company standards.
2. Automated Claim & Tone Detection with Clause Citations
When an unverified claim is detected (such as "100% compliance guaranteed"), OmniGuard AI doesn't just flag an error—it cites the exact rule from the company brand manual (e.g., Clause 3.2: Unsupported Absolute Guarantee). This gives creators transparent, clear explanations for why changes are needed.
3. Intent-Preserving Smart Micro-Patches (One-Click Fixes)
Other tools regenerate the entire document or wipe out human creativity. OmniGuard AI creates surgical, targeted replacements (such as changing "guarantees 100% compliance" to "helps achieve up to 94% compliance across enterprise workflows*"). Clicking "Apply All Fixes" replaces the text in real time and immediately updates the compliance score.
4. Exact Color Matching & Text Readability Checking
Using computer vision, the platform inspects uploaded ad graphics:
• Brand Color Accuracy: Extracts dominant colors from the image and confirms they match official brand hex codes.
• Text Contrast & Accessibility: Verifies that foreground text is clearly readable against background colors using modern web accessibility guidelines (APCA / WCAG 3.0).
5. Brand Guidelines Knowledge Base (18 Pre-Indexed Manuals)
Includes a searchable database of 18 pre-loaded enterprise guidelines covering social media, press releases, technical ads, email marketing, and legal disclosures. Teams can also upload and index custom brand manuals (.pdf, .txt, .md) directly into the platform.
6. Dedicated Pages & Smooth Navigation for Every Tab
Every platform module has its own dedicated web address (/scanner, /guidelines, /governance, /analytics, /graph). Users can bookmark pages, use browser Back and Forward buttons, and switch between views with fluid directional animations without losing their current draft.
7. Interactive A/B Comparison & Quick Keyboard Shortcuts
Creators can open a side-by-side comparison modal to see their original draft right next to the compliant version. Power users can press Ctrl+K or Cmd+K to open the command palette and run audits or switch views in a split second.

4. Team Review, Provenance & System Architecture

In a real enterprise, no single person approves an ad alone. OmniGuard AI includes a dedicated review hub that coordinates sign-offs across different departments and creates proof of compliance.

Multi-Role Governance & Department Sign-Offs
Department Role What They Are Responsible For Their Sign-Off Action in OmniGuard AI
Brand Director Visual styling, logo placement, color fidelity, and tone. Approves brand consistency or requests creative revision.
General Counsel Legal claims, substantiation fact sheets, and disclaimers. Approves legal claims, rejects false superlatives, or escalates risks.
Security Architect Data privacy, AI disclosures, and provenance verification. Confirms AI usage disclosure and verifies cryptographic integrity.
Content Creator Initial draft creation, channel formatting, and patch review. Submits drafts for review and accepts 1-click micro-patches.
Cryptographic AI Provenance Certificates

Once an asset is approved by the required departments, OmniGuard AI generates an official, tamper-proof Certificate of Compliance:

  • Digital Proof: The certificate locks together the final copy, approved image colors, cited rules, and reviewer signatures into a unique digital fingerprint.
  • Instant Verification: Anyone can take the certificate's verification code and paste it into the on-platform verifier to confirm that the asset was officially signed off and hasn't been altered.
  • Easy Export: Certificates can be viewed and printed directly from the browser or exported as structured data.
5. How the Platform is Built (Simple Architecture)

We built OmniGuard AI using modern, reliable technologies so that it runs fast, stays responsive, and never loses creator work:

Component Technology Used What It Does in Simple Terms
Web Frontend Next.js 16, React, Tailwind CSS Powers the responsive web interface with dedicated tab URLs, live score updates, and smooth button hover animations.
Backend Engine Python FastAPI, Uvicorn A high-speed server that handles copy checking, image color analysis, patch generation, and certificate verification in under a third of a second.
Database Ledger SQLite (15 Organized Tables) Safely stores 18 brand manuals, historical audit records, department sign-off logs, and certificate records without needing complex cloud setups.
Computer Vision OpenCV Accurately analyzes colors and contrast in uploaded graphics to make sure text is readable and colors match brand palettes.
Offline Fallback Client-Side Rules Engine Allows creators to keep scanning copy and applying fixes even if the network slows down or disconnects.
